Do the members of a cluster compute different functions of the input once their on-site potentials differ? Under the old rule they were exact clones. The decisive experiment drives every neuron with one scalar input and compares three arms with matched attachments: heterogeneous nonlinear potentials, identical nonlinear potentials, and linear neurons. A second question is how the eigenfunctions of the population's response operator relate to the cluster prototypes and to the eigenmodes of the input covariance.
