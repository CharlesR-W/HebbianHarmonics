How many neurons does a cluster get, and is that a law $n(\lambda)$ in the variance $\lambda$ of the mode it represents? Three cases are kept apart: in the base model, where allocation is set by the fixed attachments and the fusion scale; with plastic attachments under the same energy, which in the prototypes drifted to the quietest inputs; and with an additional principle, labelled as such, whose mass law is water-filling, $q_a = (\lambda_a - \mu)_+$. The open question is which ingredient, if any, turns that law for mass into a law for neuron counts.
