What can be recovered about the input statistics from the weights alone? A live edge reports a distance, $D_{ij}^2 = 2(b - \alpha w_{ij})$; a dead edge only says $D_{ij}^2 \ge 2b$. For linear neurons the identifiable part is the covariance on the contrast space, $PCP$ with $P = I - \mathbf 1\mathbf 1^\top/B$: the chapter asks how much of it the live edges determine, how well it is recovered, and what changes once the neurons are nonlinear.
