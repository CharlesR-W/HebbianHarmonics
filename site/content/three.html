The maths: why rows of $H$ are probability vectors

Kirchhoff at interior node $i$: $\sum_j w_{ij}(u_j - u_i) = 0$, so $u_i$ is a weighted average of its neighbours. Hence $u = L_{II}^{-1}W_{IB}\,g$, every entry of $H$ is non-negative, and $H\mathbf 1 = \mathbf 1$.
