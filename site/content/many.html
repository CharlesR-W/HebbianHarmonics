With up to about two dozen inputs (planted blocks, hierarchies, power-law spectra): how do the number of clusters and their diameter depend on the fusion scale $b$, do they show hysteresis when $b$ is swept up and then down, and is the refinement nested? Three statements are kept apart: the law for the distance between frozen responses, $D^2 = \sum_a \lambda_a (\Delta h_a)^2$; when an edge first comes alive from the uncoupled state; and how fusion, which moves the responses it depends on, shifts that point.
