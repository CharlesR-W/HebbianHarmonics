Neurons that fire together wire together

Hebbian learning is purely local: no teacher, no global loss handed down. Yet it works. So what is it computing?

  • Each neuron is a mixture of the inputs: its harmonic embedding.
  • Hebb's rule groups neurons by their embeddings, into Perron parts of the input covariance, not its eigenvectors.
  • You can read the input statistics back out of the weights.
  • With nonlinear neurons, the geometry survives; the hoped-for expansion code does not appear.

Charles R-W

Why this model? Linear rate neurons with local, correlation-driven weight updates are the standard setting for theories of Hebbian learning. Pehlevan & Chklovskii showed that Hebbian/anti-Hebbian networks of this kind follow from similarity-matching objectives (2015, 2018), and resistor networks with local learning rules are studied, and built, as physical learning machines (Kendall et al. 2020, Stern et al. 2021). The BCM model is a different rule, but its sliding threshold plays the role our regulariser does: it keeps Hebbian growth from running away.

The brain as a resistor network

Neurons are nodes with a voltage, synapses are conductances, and a few input neurons are driven by the rest of the brain.

\[ \text{neuron } i:\ u_i \quad \text{synapse}:\ w_{ij} \quad \text{input}:\ g_b \]

  • Symmetric and linear: the cleanest case, and the one the main results are about.
  • Nonlinear neurons come at the end.
  • Directed networks: plausibly a directed Laplacian (open).

Each neuron is a mixture of the inputs

In a linear network every neuron's voltage is a fixed blend of the input voltages, set by the weights alone. The blend is a point in a triangle whose corners are the inputs.

derived

\[ u_i = \textstyle\sum_b H_{ib}\, g_b = h_i \cdot g \]

  • Row \(h_i\) is a probability vector over the inputs: the neuron's harmonic embedding.
  • Where a neuron is in the wiring tells you nothing; where it is in the triangle tells you everything it computes.

Hebb's rule acts on embeddings

Two neurons covary exactly as much as their embeddings overlap, weighted by the input covariance. So "fire together" is a statement about embeddings.

derived

\[ \mathrm{Cov}(u_i, u_j) = h_i^{\top} \Sigma_g\, h_j \]

\[ \dot w_{ij} = \eta\,\big(h_i^{\top} \Sigma_g\, h_j - \theta\big) - \lambda\, w_{ij} \]

  • The note's covariance rule with a regulariser: a synapse grows when the pair covaries by more than \(\theta\), otherwise it decays to a small floor.
  • Similar listeners wire together; dissimilar ones disconnect.
