With three inputs and a handful of interior neurons: is the learned state the one the theory predicts, $w_{ij} = (b - D_{ij}^2/2)_+/\alpha$, with $D_{ij}$ the distance between two neurons' response functions, and how do neurons fuse into clusters as the fusion scale $b$ grows? The calibration case is two neurons, where the weight dynamics can already be bistable. The linear case will be drawn in the triangle of inputs, the nonlinear case by classical multidimensional scaling of $D$.
